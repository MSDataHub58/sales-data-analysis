# 🍫 Awesome Chocolates – Sales Data Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **Awesome Chocolates sales data** to identify important business trends, sales performance, and actionable insights.

The analysis was created using **Power BI** with interactive dashboards, visualizations, KPIs, insights, and recommendations.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and box volume
* Identify monthly sales trends
* Compare sales performance across teams and salespeople
* Analyze product/category performance
* Identify important patterns and trends
* Generate business insights and actionable recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and analysis
* **Power BI** – Data visualization and interactive dashboard
* **GitHub** – Project documentation and portfolio

## 📈 Dashboard

The main dashboard includes:

* Total Sales
* Total Boxes
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Monthly Box Volume
* Sales Performance by Salesperson
* Box Volume by Month and Category
* Product Filtering
* Interactive Year Filters
* Sales vs. Volume Analysis

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. February Sales Peak

February shows a significant increase in sales and box volume compared with most other months, indicating strong seasonal demand.

### 2. Top Sales Performers

Several salespeople from the **Delish** team contribute strongly to overall sales, with some salespeople generating high revenue despite selling fewer boxes.

### 3. Sales and Volume Relationship

Sales revenue generally moves with box volume, suggesting that higher sales volume is an important driver of overall revenue.

![Key Business Insights](images/insights.png)

## 🚀 Recommendations

### 1. Capitalize on Off-Peak Months

Use targeted promotions, seasonal bundles, and special offers during lower-performing months to improve baseline sales.

### 2. Upsell High-Value Products

Analyze the strategies of high-performing salespeople who generate strong revenue with lower box volumes and apply those approaches across the sales team.

### 3. Improve Inventory Planning

Prepare additional inventory and production capacity before high-demand periods to reduce the risk of stock shortages while maintaining efficient inventory levels.
